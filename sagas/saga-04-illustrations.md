# Saga 4 — "The Backfill" · Five Illustrative Plates

**Visual language:** cyan Flux-Foundation palette (#22d3ee on near-black #0a0e14), hyprland radial glow, post-quantum geometry. Hash fingerprints rendered as glowing hex-glyph constellations. Mood: cathedral-quiet, mathematical, reverent.

---

### Plate I — "The Silent Newcomer"
A single small node-orb, dim and hollow, hovers at the edge of a vast dark mesh. The older nodes blaze with dense interior light — rings of stored history layered like tree-growth. The newcomer holds only one bright point at its center (the present tip) and nothing behind it. Thin gossip-threads of cyan stream *past* it, never *into* it. Negative space dominates: the gap is the subject. Caption glyph: a node with an empty spiral where memory should be.

### Plate II — "Naming by Mathematics" (Content Addressing)
Close-up: a block of raw bytes — a chaotic cube of shifting data — collapses through a funnel of light into a single, crystalline hash-fingerprint floating above it. The fingerprint is a 64-glyph hex constellation, each character carved in cyan, locked and immutable. To one side, a duplicate block whose bytes *don't* match is shown dissolving into black static — rejected, cast into the dark. Motto etched faintly: *verify, then trust.*

### Plate III — "The Honest Archivist" (BlockStore + SyncManifest)
An architectural interior: a towering library-vault where each shelf-slot is a verified block glowing cyan, and empty slots are outlined in dashed red — the SyncManifest's list of *what is missing*. A robed, faceless archivist-figure made of flowing hash-glyphs holds a ledger that reads only one question in luminous script: **"What don't I have yet?"** Believes nothing it cannot prove.

### Plate IV — "The Flood" (64-MiB BackfillCodec channel)
The pivotal action plate. A single point-to-point conduit — a thick cyan pipe carved straight through a lattice of libp2p geometry — connects an elder node to the newcomer. History pours through it not as a trickle but as a luminous torrent of stacked blocks, each stamped with its hash. The "64 MiB" cap rendered as a wide gateway-aperture. Beside it, the 1M-context distributed compiler watches as a vast translucent eye-graph, ripples passing across every crate.

### Plate V — "Same Horizon · Divergence Zero"
Resolution. The once-hollow newcomer now blazes with the same layered interior history as the elders — indistinguishable, equal. All nodes face one direction toward a shared luminous tip on the horizon. Across the bottom, in clean monospace: `divergence: 0`. Far in the corner, a tiny rising counter — an Epsilon miner's balance ticking upward, quiet and immutable. The memory was not copied. It was *proven.*
